AleDrogi Chat is a buyer-support panel for Allegro sellers. It gathers messages,
discussions and complaints in one place and takes the repetitive part of replying
off your hands — including when nobody is sitting at the computer.

## Who it's for

Companies selling on Allegro whose buyer support has started eating the day: the
same questions about shipping, chasing rating requests by hand, complaints getting
lost in the message centre.

The app runs **on your own hardware** — a mini-PC in the office or a cheap VPS.
Conversation history and buyer data stay with you, not in someone else's cloud.

## What it does

**One place for conversations.** Every thread in a single panel, split into ordinary
messages and discussions/complaints. The colour of a bubble tells you who wrote it:
incoming messages are white, manual replies green, and autoresponder replies amber
with an "Auto" label.

**Autoresponders.** A rule is a condition plus a template: "always", "first message
in a thread", or a specific keyword. Each rule works in one of two modes — **Auto**
sends by itself, **Suggested** only offers a ready reply above the text box and a
human presses send.

**Post-sale workflow.** A six-stage sequence walks the buyer through the order: paid
→ awaiting dispatch → in transit → out for delivery → received → rating request.
Each stage has its own mode: automatic, suggestion, or fully manual.

**Templates with variables.** Placeholders such as buyer, order number and product
fill themselves in from order data. Some variables are picked by a human from a list
before sending — deliberately, for the trickier cases.

**Leads.** Questions asked before a purchase are flagged separately. A lead disappears
on its own once an order shows up, and counts as a conversion.

**Reports.** Complaints: how many resolved, how quickly, with what outcome and around
which products. Leads: how many active, how many converted.

## The principles behind it

:::tip[People come first]
Once someone takes over a thread, automation stays out of it. Rules for complaints
and discussions suggest a reply by default rather than sending one — the decision
is always a person's.
:::

The app works in the background on a steady polling cycle. No browser needs to be
open for auto-replies and order stages to keep moving.
